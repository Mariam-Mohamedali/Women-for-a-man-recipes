Steak fajita power bowls

Description

Steak fajita power bowls are a nutritious, high-protein meal featuring seared, marinated flank or sirloin steak, sautéed bell peppers, and onions served over a base of rice, quinoa, or cauliflower rice.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound lean steak, cut into strips.
  • ½ cup fresh lime juice.
  • ½ tablespoon ground black pepper.
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro.
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil.
  • 1 large onion, cut into thin strips.
  • 1 julienned green bell pepper.
  • 2 lemons, quartered.

Preparation Steps

  1. Marinate the Steak: Combine olive oil, lime juice, and spices (cumin, chili powder, oregano, garlic powder) in a bowl. Add sliced or whole steak, coating thoroughly. Let it sit for at least 15-30 minutes, or up to 8 hours for better flavor.
  2. Prepare the Rice: While the steak marinates, cook rice according to package directions. Once cooked, stir in fresh lime juice, chopped cilantro, and salt to taste.
  3. Cook the Veggies: In a large skillet or cast-iron pan, heat oil over medium-high heat. Add sliced peppers and onions, sautéing for 5-7 minutes until soft but slightly crisp. Season with salt, pepper, or extra fajita seasoning, then remove from the pan.
  4. Cook the Steak: In the same pan, cook the steak for 3-5 minutes per side until it reaches your desired doneness (145°F for medium-rare). Remove from heat and let it rest for 5 minutes before slicing against the grain.
  5. Assemble the Bowl: Divide the cilantro-lime rice among bowls. Top with the sautéed peppers, onions, and sliced steak.
  6. Garnish and Serve: Add guacamole, salsa, black beans, or a lime crema (sour cream mixed with lime juice and zest)

Turkey lavash wraps

Description

Turkey lavash wraps are a light, flexible Middle Eastern flatbread (lavash) filled with deli turkey, fresh vegetables (spinach, cucumber, tomatoes), cheese, and spreads like hummus or avocado

Ingredients

  • Lavash: 1 sheet of fresh lavash bread.
  • Protein: Sliced deli turkey (smoked or plain).
  • Creamy Element (Binder): Cream cheese, Greek yogurt, or mayonnaise.
  • Vegetables: Romaine lettuce, arugula, spinach, sliced cucumbers, or roasted red peppers.
  • Flavor Boosters: Goat cheese, fresh dill, scallions, lemon juice, or chopped walnuts.

Preparation Steps

    Step 1
  1. In a medium bowl, combine scallion, goat cheese, yogurt, and 2 teaspoons oil; season with salt and pepper.
    2. Step 2
  2. In a large bowl, gently toss arugula, dill, lemon juice, and remaining 1 teaspoon oil to combine.
    3. Step 3
  3. On a work surface, lay out lavash. Spread goat cheese mixture in an even layer over bread, leaving a 1" border on all sides. Divide turkey, red peppers, cucumbers, and arugula salad among wraps.
    4. Step 4
  4. Pull one half of lavash up and over filling like a burrito, then tightly wrap to enclose. Tuck in ends to seal. Cut in half before serving.

Bon Appetit!

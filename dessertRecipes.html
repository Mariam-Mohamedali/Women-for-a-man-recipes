Kunafa with Pistachio

Description

Description

Kunafa is a traditional Middle Eastern dessert believed to date back to the Fatimid Caliphate. It is famous for its crispy layers and sweet flavor, often enjoyed during Ramadan and celebrations.

Ingredients

  • 250g Kunafa dough (shredded)
  • 100g unsalted butter (melted)
  • 200g sweet cheese or clotted cream
  • 100g crushed pistachios for topping
  • 200g sugar
  • 120ml water
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice (for sugar syrup)
  • 1 teaspoon rose water or orange blossom water (optional, for syrup)

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Prepare sugar syrup: boil sugar and water with lemon juice for 5-7 minutes, then add rose/orange blossom water. Let it cool.
  3. Mix shredded kunafa dough with melted butter.
  4. Grease a baking tray and spread half of the dough evenly.
  5. Add the cheese or cream filling on top of the dough.
  6. Cover with the remaining dough and press gently.
  7. Bake in the oven for 25-30 minutes until golden and crispy.
  8. Remove from oven and pour sugar syrup evenly over the hot kunafa.
  9. Garnish with crushed pistachios and serve warm.

Bon Appetit!

Umm Ali

Description

Umm Ali is a famous Egyptian dessert that dates back to the Mamluk era. It is a warm, creamy pastry pudding enjoyed especially during Winter .

Ingredients

  • 200g puff pastry or phyllo dough
  • 1 liter milk
  • 150g sugar
  • 100g mixed nuts (almonds, pistachios, cashews)
  • 50g raisins
  • 50g desiccated coconut
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 50g butter

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Break the puff pastry into small pieces and bake lightly with butter until golden.
  3. In a saucepan, heat milk with sugar and vanilla until warm.
  4. Place baked pastry pieces in a baking dish.
  5. Sprinkle nuts, raisins, and coconut over the pastry.
  6. Pour the warm milk mixture over everything.
  7. Bake in the oven for 20-25 minutes until the top is slightly golden and milk is absorbed.
  8. Serve warm, optionally topped with extra nuts or cinnamon.

Bon Appetit!
